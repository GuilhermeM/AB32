### 🧠 Gentle Training that Works

This collar helps teach your dog to stay in their safe area:

1. 📢 If they go too far, it beeps softly.

2. ⚡ Then gives a small static zap if they keep going.

3. ⬆️ The zap gets stronger, but only a little at a time.

4. 🛑 After 3 tries, it stops so your dog isn’t over-corrected.

No harm. No stress. Just smart, simple training.
